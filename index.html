01 / The idea

Good design has a point of view.
Your code should, too.

KD INDUSTRIES gives AI a design brief it can actually use: visual rules, useful constraints, and a clear sense of what belongs.

The standout feature · Study

Learn the feel.
Not the pixels.

Give KD INDUSTRIES a screenshot or URL. It studies the design DNA behind it — layout rhythm, type hierarchy, spacing, color relationships, and component patterns — without tracing the original screen.

Then use those principles to build with your own content, or export a portable design guide your tools can follow anywhere.
